# Booking Bridge — 30-Second Marketing Video

Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, 24–30 seconds  
Audience: independent Japanese salon owners already serving—or wanting to serve—international customers  
CTA: view the NAGI demo, then reply for a short private audit

## Storyboard

| Time | Visual | On-screen copy | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | A visitor finds a salon on Instagram, then pauses at a Japanese-only booking page. | `海外のお客様、予約前に迷っていませんか？` | “International guests often find the salon—but stop before booking.” |
| 3–7s | Freeze on three questions: services, first visit, booking method. | `Menu? First visit? How to book?` | “The questions are simple: what can I book, what should I expect, and where do I continue?” |
| 7–13s | Reveal the two-phone Booking Bridge campaign visual. Slow push-in. | `Keep your website.` / `Add one clearer way to book.` | “Booking Bridge adds one clear bilingual path without replacing your website.” |
| 13–19s | Quick screen sequence: service menu → first-visit guide → booking choices. | `Understand → Feel ready → Continue` | “Guests understand the service, feel ready for their first visit, and continue to the system you already use.” |
| 19–24s | Show LINE, Hot Pepper and salon-form handoff. | `LINE · Hot Pepper · Current form` | “No second calendar. No duplicate reservation management.” |
| 24–30s | Product card with price, timeframe and CTA. | `Booking Bridge Kit` / `¥45,000 · 7–10 days` / `30秒デモを見る` | “A fixed-scope Booking Bridge is forty-five thousand yen. See the thirty-second demo.” |

## Edit Direction

- Use warm ivory, forest green and coral orange throughout.
- Use the generated campaign visual as the main reveal at 7–13 seconds.
- Keep every caption within the center 80% safe area for Reels and Shorts.
- Use calm, precise motion: 105% push-ins, short crossfades and one clean cursor/tap animation.
- Avoid fast glitch transitions, generic stock footage and unverified promises about increasing bookings.
- Label NAGI as a concept demonstration wherever the name appears.

## Caption

Your salon may already welcome international guests. The booking path may be the part that still feels difficult.

Booking Bridge adds a clear bilingual guide for services, first-visit details and the next booking step—then hands the guest to LINE, Hot Pepper or the form your team already uses.

Fixed scope: ¥45,000 · 7–10 working days.
